Construction Workshop · Problem Statement

Project mobilisation & statutory permissions

USN 01FE23BCV002 · Roll No. 251 · Batch B4

1. Problem Statement

Establish a controlled project-start process: confirm land and project records, identify the competent planning/building authority, prepare coordinated architectural/structural/services drawings, track statutory submissions, establish site access and temporary facilities, and maintain a responsibility matrix. The approval route must be confirmed against the project location, land-use status, building classification and current local requirements.

3. Proposed Engineering Solution / Process

Prepare an approvals register with authority, submission, responsible person, target date, status and evidence reference. Sequence mobilisation only after required permissions and safety arrangements are confirmed. Use a site logistics plan for gates, material entry, emergency access, storage, utilities and worker facilities.

Site visit / engineer interaction: [Add only if actually conducted. Identify the site and date, describe the observation, and record the engineer's role and discussion accurately.]

4. Reflection

A clear approval and mobilisation sequence reduces avoidable stoppages, redesign and interface conflicts. The team should distinguish statutory approval from internal technical review and retain dated evidence for each decision.

5. Conclusion

A project should move from feasibility to mobilisation through documented approvals, coordinated drawings, assigned responsibilities and a verified site-establishment plan.
