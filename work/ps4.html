Construction Workshop · Problem Statement

Steel & formwork wastage

USN 01FE23BCV002 · Roll No. 251 · Batch B4

1. Problem Statement

Reduce reinforcement offcuts, incorrect bar bending, damaged formwork and avoidable concrete loss while maintaining drawing compliance, safety and required structural detailing.

3. Proposed Engineering Solution / Process

Prepare and check bar bending schedules against the latest approved drawings; group cutting lengths to optimise stock bars; record theoretical and issued quantities; segregate reusable offcuts only where permitted; inspect formwork for dimensions, tightness, stability and release-agent application; measure wastage by work package and investigate variances.

Site visit / engineer interaction: [Add only if actually conducted. Identify the site and date, describe the observation, and record the engineer's role and discussion accurately.]

4. Reflection

Wastage reduction is not simply minimising material use. Rebar must not be shortened, substituted or re-bent without approval. Formwork reuse must not compromise dimensions, finish, stability or worker safety.

5. Conclusion

Drawing-controlled BBS, planned cutting, careful handling, formwork inspection and measured wastage tracking can reduce cost without compromising structural quality.
