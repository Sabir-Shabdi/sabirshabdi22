Construction Workshop · Problem Statement

Stores, procurement & quality control

USN 01FE23BCV002 · Roll No. 251 · Batch B4

1. Problem Statement

Plan procurement and storage for cement, aggregates, reinforcement, formwork and other construction materials so that materials are available when required, conform to specifications and remain traceable from receipt to use.

3. Proposed Engineering Solution / Process

Use approved material submittals, vendor evaluation, purchase orders, delivery inspection, test certificates, sampling and acceptance records. Store cement in a dry raised location with batch-wise FIFO control; segregate reinforcement by diameter and grade; prevent aggregate contamination; maintain stock, issue, return and rejection registers.

Site visit / engineer interaction: [Add only if actually conducted. Identify the site and date, describe the observation, and record the engineer's role and discussion accurately.]

4. Reflection

Material quality is not established by appearance alone. Inspection, test evidence, traceability and proper storage are essential. Poor procurement timing can interrupt work, while excess stock increases handling damage and working-capital cost.

5. Conclusion

A systematic procurement and stores-control process supports quality, continuity, traceability and cost control throughout construction.
